# Refund, Cancellation, and Payment Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Refund, Cancellation, and Payment Policy applies to Autoscape quote payments and related billing flows. Autoscape is operated by 1001283716 ONTARIO INC., operating as Autoscape.

This is a legal draft for attorney review and does not guarantee legal compliance.

## Quotes and Approved Payments

Instant quote values are estimates until reviewed and approved by Autoscape. A quote request does not create a confirmed booking or final invoice by itself.

Approved payment pages may offer:

- A seasonal payment for the approved discounted seasonal total.
- A weekly per-visit payment plan, processed through Stripe as recurring billing and capped at the approved visit count.

Prices are shown in Canadian dollars and include applicable taxes unless otherwise shown.

## Payment Processor

Payments are processed by Stripe. Autoscape does not store full card details on the website. Stripe may store and process payment information according to its own terms and privacy notices.

Autoscape stores payment-related records such as Stripe customer ID, checkout session ID, payment intent ID, subscription ID, payment status, paid invoice IDs, amount, currency, recurring interval, card brand, last four digits, and expiry summary where provided by Stripe.

## Seasonal Plan Refunds

For the seasonal plan, a customer may receive a full refund up to 24 hours after the first visit.

After that 24-hour post-first-visit window, seasonal payments are not refundable except where required by applicable law or expressly approved by Autoscape in writing.

## Weekly or Per-Visit Cancellations

For weekly/per-visit billing, customers may cancel before the next unpaid scheduled visit. Completed visits and already-started work remain payable.

If Stripe subscription billing has started, cancellation may require Autoscape or Stripe Billing Portal action. Billing stops according to the approved plan, subscription settings, and any cancellation confirmed by Autoscape or Stripe.

## Rescheduling

Autoscape may reschedule service because of weather, wet turf, unsafe conditions, equipment availability, access issues, route changes, municipal restrictions, or other operational needs.

Rescheduling does not automatically create a refund unless the applicable plan terms, approved quote, law, or Autoscape written approval provides one.

## Scope Changes and Site Conditions

Final pricing may change if:

- The submitted lawn boundary is inaccurate.
- Autoscape identifies missing mowable areas, unsafe areas, obstacles, slopes, drainage issues, access limits, or other site conditions.
- The customer requests a different scope.
- Required permissions, access, or site readiness are not available.
- Autoscape and the customer agree to additional work or revised pricing.

Autoscape should document material scope changes in the approved quote, service agreement, invoice, or written communication.

## Failed Payments and Billing Disputes

Payment failures may delay or stop service. Customers should contact Autoscape promptly at contact@autoscape.ca if a payment is incorrect, duplicated, disputed, or appears fraudulent.

Chargebacks or disputes may result in suspension of service while the issue is reviewed.
