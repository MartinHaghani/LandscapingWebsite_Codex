## Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information generated by the website and quote tools, and information received from service providers.

Information you provide may include:

- Name, email address, phone number, and contact messages.
- Service address or address search text.
- Quote ID, account profile details, and billing preference.
- Information submitted through the contact form, complete profile flow, claim quote flow, dashboard, or payment surfaces.
- Email marketing consent if you choose to opt in.

Quote and property information may include:

- Geocoded address coordinates returned by Mapbox.
- Lawn boundary polygons, obstacle polygons, edit history in the local quote draft, area, perimeter, service frequency, billing mode, and pricing results.
- Service-area request details, including address, coordinates, source, and whether the location was inside the service area when captured.
- Admin-reviewed quote details, price overrides, notes, status changes, payment status, approved map preview links, and audit records.

Account and payment information may include:

- Clerk account identifiers, email, name, phone number, authentication state, and profile metadata.
- Stripe Checkout session IDs, Stripe customer IDs, payment intent or subscription IDs, payment status, paid invoice IDs, card brand, last four digits, and card expiry summary when available from Stripe.
- Autoscape does not store full card numbers through this website.

Technical, analytics, and attribution information may include:

- Google Ads click identifiers, UTM parameters, landing path, referrer, device type, browser summary, and conversion events.
- Browser local storage and session storage values used to restore quote drafts and attribution snapshots.
- IP address used for rate limiting and, for admin actions, a hashed IP address and user-agent string in audit logs.
- Cookies or similar technologies from Clerk, Google, Mapbox, Stripe, and other service providers.

## How We Collect Information

We collect information when you:

- Visit the website.
- Search for an address.
- Draw or edit a lawn boundary.
- Submit a quote draft.
- Create, sign in to, or update an account.
- Claim a quote.
- Contact Autoscape.
- Request service-area expansion.
- Open a payment page or start Stripe Checkout.
- Opt in to email marketing.

Some information is collected automatically by browser storage, cookies, server logs, third-party scripts, or service providers.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate the website and quote system.
- Check whether an address is inside the current service area.
- Measure lawn geometry, calculate quote values, and support human review.
- Create and manage customer accounts, quote ownership, dashboards, and profile requirements.
- Communicate about quotes, account activity, review status, approved payment links, scheduling, service questions, and support.
- Process payments through Stripe.
- Send transactional approved-quote emails through Resend.
- Measure advertising performance and quote submissions.
- Maintain audit logs, detect misuse, enforce rate limits, and protect quote integrity.
- Improve the service area, quote workflow, and customer experience.
- Send email marketing only where Autoscape has recorded consent or another lawful basis confirmed by legal review.
