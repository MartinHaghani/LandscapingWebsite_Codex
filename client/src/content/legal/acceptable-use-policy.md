# Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to the Autoscape website, forms, accounts, quote tools, dashboard, admin-facing workflows, and payment links. Autoscape is operated by 1001283716 ONTARIO INC., operating as Autoscape.

This is a legal draft for attorney review and does not guarantee legal compliance.

## Permitted Use

You may use the website to learn about Autoscape, check service availability, submit contact requests, create and manage your account, draw residential lawn boundaries, request a quote, claim a quote, and pay for an approved quote.

## Prohibited Use

You must not:

- Submit false, misleading, unlawful, or unauthorized property information.
- Submit a quote for a property you do not own, lease, manage, or have authority to act for.
- Interfere with maps, quote validation, payment links, account security, or admin systems.
- Attempt to access another customer's quote, account, payment link, or personal information.
- Circumvent rate limits, authentication, authorization, or payment controls.
- Upload, paste, or transmit malicious code, spam, phishing, or harmful content.
- Use the website to harass, abuse, threaten, or impersonate anyone.
- Scrape, copy, reverse engineer, or overload the website in a way that harms Autoscape or other users.
- Use the website for unlawful, unsafe, or unauthorized activities.

## User Submissions

The current website does not provide a general public posting feature or photo-upload feature. It does allow users to submit contact messages, address information, quote geometry, account profile details, and quote/payment interactions.

You are responsible for your submissions and for having authority to provide them.

## Enforcement

Autoscape may restrict access, reject submissions, revoke payment links, decline service, preserve evidence, or contact appropriate parties where misuse, fraud, security risk, property-authority concerns, or unlawful activity is suspected.

## Reporting Misuse

Report suspected misuse to contact@autoscape.ca.
