## Launch Region

Autoscape's launch availability is centred on Vaughan and Maple, Ontario. High-level public region labels and service-area maps are informational. A specific address must be checked through the website or confirmed by Autoscape.

## Approximate Coverage

The displayed service-area overlay is approximate and intentionally obfuscated for station privacy. It should not be treated as a precise boundary, guarantee, or promise of service.

Address-level availability may depend on:

- Distance to active base stations.
- Route density and operational capacity.
- Property access and safety.
- Equipment suitability.
- Local rules and restrictions.
- Weather, seasonal conditions, and scheduling.
- Human review by Autoscape.

## Out-of-Area Requests

If an address is outside the active area, Autoscape may log the location to help plan future expansion. Logging a location does not guarantee that Autoscape will add the area or contact the requester.

## No Service Guarantee

Autoscape may decline, postpone, or condition service for any property where the service area, route, property layout, equipment suitability, access, safety, or business capacity does not support service.
