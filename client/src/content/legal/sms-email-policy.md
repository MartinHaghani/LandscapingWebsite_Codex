## Transactional Communications

Autoscape may send or initiate transactional communications about:

- Contact form responses.
- Quote submissions, review status, and approved quote emails.
- Account, profile, and dashboard activity.
- Payment links, Stripe checkout status, billing portal access, payment failures, and receipts or invoice-related messages.
- Scheduling, property access, safety, service changes, weather delays, support, and customer service.

Approved-quote transactional emails are currently sent through Resend when configured.

## Email Marketing

The website may collect optional consent to receive Autoscape updates and offers by email. This consent must be optional and unchecked by default.

Autoscape should not send promotional email campaigns unless it has recorded consent or another lawful basis confirmed by legal review. Commercial email messages should include Autoscape identification information and a working unsubscribe method.

## SMS Marketing

Autoscape does not currently collect website consent for SMS marketing and does not currently operate SMS marketing through the codebase reviewed for this draft.

SMS marketing should not be launched until Autoscape has separate consent language, message-frequency disclosures, STOP/HELP handling, sender identification, provider configuration, and legal review.

## Calls and Direct Contact

If you provide a phone number, Autoscape may use it for transactional service, quote, scheduling, account, or support purposes. Autoscape should not use phone numbers for promotional SMS campaigns unless separate legal and technical requirements are satisfied.

## Your Choices

You can contact Autoscape at contact@autoscape.ca to ask about communication preferences. Email marketing opt-out requests should be honored through the unsubscribe process once marketing campaigns are active.
