# Estimate, Quote, and Booking Terms

Last updated: May 1, 2026

## Instant Estimate Flow

The instant quote flow asks you to:

- Enter or select a service address.
- Confirm service-area eligibility.
- Draw service polygons for mowable lawn areas.
- Draw obstacle polygons for excluded areas.
- Review measured area, perimeter, plan, and pricing.
- Select seasonal or weekly/per-visit billing where available.
- Submit the quote for review.

The website may save a local quote draft in your browser until you submit or clear it.

## Measurement and Pricing

The system measures geometry using geodesic-safe tools and the server revalidates submitted quote metrics. Pricing uses the approved pricing formula in the codebase, including base fee, area, perimeter, distance to station, minimum charge, weekly visit count, and seasonal discount.

Autoscape may adjust quote geometry and pricing after review.

## Human Review

Submitted quotes enter Autoscape review. Autoscape may accept, reject, revise, or request more information. Review may consider mowable boundaries, obstacles, property access, safety, route planning, service-area eligibility, and equipment suitability.

## Booking

A booking is not confirmed until Autoscape approves the quote and any required payment, access, scheduling, and service conditions are satisfied.

Payment alone may not guarantee a specific service date if weather, safety, access, or operational constraints require rescheduling.

## Customer Authority

You must have authority to request landscaping services for the residential property. Tenants and property managers are responsible for having owner, landlord, condominium, HOA, or other required permission.

## Scope Changes

If site conditions, customer requests, access constraints, unsafe areas, hidden hazards, or inaccurate submitted geometry require a scope change, Autoscape may revise the quote, reschedule, or decline service.

## Payment Terms

Prices are shown in Canadian dollars and include applicable taxes unless otherwise shown. Stripe processes online payment. Seasonal and weekly/per-visit billing terms are described in the Refund, Cancellation, and Payment Policy.
