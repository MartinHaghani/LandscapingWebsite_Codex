# Accessibility Statement

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Autoscape wants its website to be usable by customers with diverse access needs. Autoscape is operated by 1001283716 ONTARIO INC., operating as Autoscape.

This is a legal draft for attorney review and does not guarantee legal compliance or claim any specific WCAG conformance level.

## Our Approach

Autoscape aims to support accessible website use through:

- Semantic page structure where practical.
- Keyboard-focus support, including a skip-to-content link.
- Form labels and visible validation messages.
- Readable contrast and responsive layouts.
- Plain-language page content.

## Known Limitations

Some website features may be difficult for certain users, including:

- Interactive maps and drawing tools.
- Third-party authentication screens from Clerk.
- Stripe-hosted checkout or billing portal pages.
- Mapbox tiles, geocoding, satellite imagery, and map previews.
- Highly visual property-boundary drawing workflows.
